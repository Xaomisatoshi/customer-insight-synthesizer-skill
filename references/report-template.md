# Product Insights Report Template

Use this template for customer interview synthesis unless the user requests another format.

# Customer Interview Product Insights

## Executive Summary
Write 3-5 bullets covering the strongest pain points, affected users, and most important product implications.

## Source Coverage
Include:
- number of interviews or notes analyzed
- customer / segment / persona if known
- Google Drive files reviewed
- related Asana tasks or projects checked
- important limitations

## Prioritized Pain Points
Use this table structure:

| Priority | Pain Point | Who / Segment | Evidence | Severity | Frequency | Confidence | Product Implication | Next Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Scoring guidance:
- Priority: P0, P1, P2, or P3
- Severity: critical, high, medium, or low
- Frequency: recurring, several, isolated, or unknown
- Confidence: high, medium, or low

## Product Opportunities
Group opportunities by type:

### Product / UX
Features, workflow improvements, interface changes, automation, integrations.

### Onboarding / Education
Documentation, help content, in-product guidance, templates, training.

### Support / Operations
Support process gaps, account management follow-ups, implementation risks.

### Research Follow-up
Questions that need additional discovery or validation.
