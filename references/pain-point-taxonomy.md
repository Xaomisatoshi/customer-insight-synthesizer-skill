# Pain Point Taxonomy

Use this taxonomy to classify interview evidence consistently.

## Workflow Friction
The user can complete the task, but it is slow, repetitive, manual, or requires too many tools.

Signals:
- repeated copy/paste
- spreadsheet workaround
- waiting on another team
- too many clicks or context switches

## Comprehension Gap
The user does not understand what to do, what happened, or what a product state means.

Signals:
- confusion
- misinterpretation
- unclear labels or flows
- high support dependency

## Capability Gap
The product cannot support a required job or edge case.

Signals:
- missing integration
- missing export/import
- unsupported role, permission, or workflow
- forced external tool usage

## Trust / Risk Concern
The user worries about correctness, compliance, security, reliability, or loss of control.

Signals:
- manual verification
- audit concerns
- reluctance to automate
- permission or data concerns

## Collaboration Breakdown
The user's work depends on coordination with others, and the product does not support that coordination well.

Signals:
- unclear ownership
- handoff failures
- status visibility gaps
- notification noise or silence

## Adoption / Value Barrier
The user does not see enough value, cannot justify switching, or cannot get others to adopt.

Signals:
- unclear ROI
- training burden
- low internal buy-in
- competing tools

## Commercial / Packaging Pain
The pain is tied to pricing, limits, procurement, plan boundaries, or perceived fairness.

Signals:
- plan limitation frustration
- procurement blockers
- unclear pricing model
- expansion resistance
